# 3K — Academic Business Plan Prompt for Perplexity (Professional Edition)

Copy and paste the entire block below into **Perplexity** (or any AI tool):

```markdown
You are a senior business educator and entrepreneurship curriculum advisor.

Generate a comprehensive, academic-grade Business Plan for a solo student venture named "3K". 

---

### VENTURE BACKGROUND & CONTEXT:
1. Brand & Positioning:
   - Brand Name: 3K
   - Identity: Modern, minimalist monochrome aesthetic (honest, accessible, jargon-free).
   - Digital Infrastructure: Live single-page responsive web platform hosted on GitHub Pages with integrated automated inquiry channels.
   - Core Domain: Tech repair, IT assistance, and academic project support.

2. Academic Course Context:
   - Course: Undergraduate Entrepreneurship (General Education / Minor Subject; not the founder's primary major).
   - Critical Requirement: The evaluation panel strictly requires a defined, tangible "PRODUCT" (not solely manual hours-for-hire services).
   - Academic Rubrics: Evaluated on Problem-Solution alignment, 4Ps Marketing Mix, Operational Feasibility for a solo student, Unit Economics, and Commercial Viability.

3. Founder Capabilities & Constraints:
   - Skill Profile: Cross-functional blend of Tech (hardware/software troubleshooting, OS setup, basic web tech), Design (UI/presentation decks, visual layouts), and Writing (academic research, technical documentation).
   - Operational Constraints: Solo founder, near-zero startup capital (₱0–₱1,000 bootstrap), no physical retail store or manufacturing inventory required, managed alongside full-time coursework.

---

### YOUR TASK:
Produce a formal, defense-ready Business Plan following standard university entrepreneurship criteria:

#### 1. THE FLAGSHIP PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
To fulfill the course requirement of a "Product", formulate a scalable Digital Product / Web-Based Tool hosted under the 3K brand that addresses common student/academic technical pain points:
- Provide 2 distinct digital product concepts:
  * Option A: An automated academic presentation and document formatting web tool/kit.
  * Option B: An interactive, self-service hardware/software diagnostic checklist & troubleshooting web application.
- Select the strongest concept, designate a formal product name, and detail its 3 core features.
- Provide the academic defense justification: Formally explain why this software/digital asset qualifies as a true "Scalable Product" (reproducible output, standardized packaging, zero marginal replication cost, decoupled from founder labor hours).

#### 2. HYBRID MONETIZATION ARCHITECTURE (Product + Service Synergy)
Articulate how 3K operates a dual-engine business model:
- Primary Engine (The Product): Tiered digital sales (one-time micro-passes, specialized template/asset export licenses, or low-cost student passes at ₱50–₱150).
- Secondary Engine (High-Touch Services): Escalation into direct 3K services (Hardware Repair, Customized IT Assistance, and In-depth Academic/Project Support).
- Conversion Funnel: How inbound product users convert into service clients and vice versa.

#### 3. COMPLETE ACADEMIC BUSINESS PLAN SECTIONS
Provide rigorous, structured write-ups for:
1. Executive Summary: Concise vision, value proposition, problem, product solution, and growth outlook.
2. Problem Statement: Real-world pain points faced by college students and young professionals (unreliable hardware, impending deadlines, complicated software, steep repair costs).
3. The 4 Ps of Marketing:
   - Product: Specifications of the digital tool and its packaged deliverables.
   - Price: Transparent student-friendly price points, markup, and gross margin analysis.
   - Place: Cloud/web distribution via GitHub/custom domain; zero physical shelf space.
   - Promotion: Cost-effective campus penetration (student networks, peer recommendations, digital communities).
4. Operations & Workflow Plan:
   - End-to-end customer journey from website visit to delivery.
   - Operational feasibility demonstrating how a solo student sustains this without academic burnout.
5. Financial Feasibility (One-Semester / 4-Month Horizon):
   - Initial capital requirements (hosting, basic utilities: ≤ ₱1,000).
   - Unit economics table (Price per unit vs direct delivery costs).
   - Breakeven point and realistic projected monthly net income.
6. Defense Preparation & Panel FAQ:
   - 3 rigorous questions an academic panel will ask (regarding competition against free alternatives, product definition legitimacy, and solo execution capacity).
   - Concise, persuasive defense responses structured for the oral presentation.

Format using clean markdown headings, structured bullet points, and realistic metrics. Ensure the tone is entirely professional, objective, and presentation-ready.
```
